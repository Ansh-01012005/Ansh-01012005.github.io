I'm Ansh, a final-year B.Tech student in Computer Science & Engineering (AI/ML) at UPES, Dehradun. My work sits at the intersection of applied machine learning and software engineering — I like taking a messy dataset and carrying it all the way to a decision someone can act on.

Recently, I built a credit risk assessment system at IBM SkillsBuild, translating XGBoost default probabilities into approve/decline decisions and explaining declines with SHAP.
